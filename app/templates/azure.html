  1. Sign in to the Azure portal as an Owner or User Access Administrator of the subscription.
  2. Open Subscriptions and pick the subscription. To cover every subscription at once, open Management groups and pick Tenant Root Group instead.
  3. Choose Access control (IAM) > Add > Add role assignment.
  4. Pick the role Cost Management Reader and press Next.
  5. Choose Select members, search for Tenvaultix 365 Health Check, select it, then Review + assign.
  6. Wait a few minutes (Microsoft can take up to an hour), then press Refresh on this page.

This role can only read costs and budgets. It can't see or change your servers, data or settings, and you can remove it at any time from the same page. If you buy Azure through a Microsoft partner (CSP), the partner has to allow customers to see costs first.
