Why every week, not once?

Your tenant keeps changing: people join and leave, someone gets admin rights, a license is bought and forgotten. After a phishing attack, the attacker quietly adds a forwarding rule. A one-time audit misses all of that.

Questions

What access do you need?

Read-only access through Microsoft's official API. Your admin approves it on Microsoft's own consent screen, which lists every permission. Scanning can't change anything in your tenant. Optional one-click fixes happen only when your admin approves that one change on Microsoft's screen. The privacy page lists each permission and why we need it.

Do you read our email or files?

No. We read settings and metadata only: users, licenses, policies, inbox-rule settings and sign-in dates. We never read email content or open documents.

Which Microsoft 365 plans work?

Business Basic, Standard and Premium, and E3/E5. A few checks (sign-in history, Conditional Access, device compliance) need Business Premium or E3/E5; on other plans the report tells you what those checks would show and how to get them.

How do we stop?

Disconnect from your dashboard and we delete your reports straight away. You can also remove our app any time in Microsoft Entra under Enterprise applications.

Is this a Microsoft product?

No. Tenvaultix 365 Health Check is an independent service from Tenvaultix that uses Microsoft's official APIs.
